7 December 2025 · Sacramento · Road

42.2 km · 2:52:23 · 4:05 per km · 188 m gain

My first marathon and technically a BQ. The training block was the most mentally and physically exhausting athletic endeavor I have ever undertaken. I struggled or failed to complete many of my prescribed workouts, including a massive effort to complete 16 miles at marathon pace, proceeded immediately by an utter failure to complete even close to the scheduled 18 miles at marathon pace the following week. Despite this, the taper worked wonders, and the race itself was fantastic. I ran a negative split, starting at ~6:50 min/mi and finishing at ~6:30 min/mi, causing me to feel great through 22 miles and easily hold on for the last few to finish strong.
